# Standing requirements

The user requires every page to be mobile friendly and built toward eligibility for Microsoft Advertising (Bing). Apply these requirements to future changes.

- Check the rendered experience at 375px and a desktop width. No horizontal overflow; readable text, keyboard access, labeled inputs, and touch targets of at least 44px for primary controls.
- Keep pricing consistent between catalogue, homepage, product views, and cart. Do not invent availability, reviews, certifications, manufacturer relationships, promotions, delivery promises, or discounts.
- Keep navigation predictable and the back button usable. No forced redirects, entry/exit popups, or unsolicited downloads.
- The current app is a frontend preview. Cart product IDs and quantities persist in local storage on the same browser; checkout is a labeled preview with no orders, payment, or newsletter delivery. Never claim it is approved or ready for paid traffic.
- Before ads: confirm business identity and contact details, original licensed product assets, prices and stock; finish real product destinations and all policy/help links; implement the intended purchase path; publish business-specific privacy, sale, shipping, return and warranty terms; deploy on an accessible HTTPS domain; review the actual ads and destination with current Microsoft policies.
- Update the privacy notice when hosting, analytics/UET, payment, or newsletter services are introduced. Do not add tracking silently.
